## Useful Context

Give as much context as you can about what you find out during the discovery here. This should be the "story" of **WHY**
we are going to do something, or **WHY** we are deciding against doing anything.

## Proposed Approach

Describe **HOW** you think we should go about arriving at a solution to the problem

### Benefits

- Outline the benefits of your proposed approach
- Ideally in concise bulleting format

### Considerations

- Outline any risks or drawbacks of your approach that are worth noting
- Each with options/plans for mitigation
- Also in concise bulleting format

### Alternatives

- There are always multiple ways to solve a problem
- List alternate approaches you researched or considered and why they're not better
- Also in concise bulleting format

## Phasing

Describe a breakdown of the major milestones/phases necessary to implement the solution, including an estimate of **WHEN** each milestone should be completed

## Ownership and Access

This should include details about **WHO** (person or team) has ownership of the software (i.e management and maintenance), and **WHO** (people or teams) should have access to it.

## Assumptions

- Things we are pretty sure about, but may need some extra validation

## Unknowns

- Things that may require additional research or validation
- Only what the reader deferred, or what the breakdown schedules as research
- Never a decision you found and chose not to raise — take those to the reader instead
